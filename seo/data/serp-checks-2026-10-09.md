## What this tells us

1. The website ranks only for its own name. For every product or category search, directories (IndiaMART, JustDial, TradeIndia) and competitors' Google Maps listings take page 1.
2. Google has indexed 4 of 22 pages. The 15 product pages and 3 blog posts aren't indexed, which matches the crawl: no links point to them.
3. Google picked the vercel.app copy as the main result for the brand: the duplicate-site problem is real, not theoretical.
4. The Google Maps listing is the single biggest local problem: misspelt, unclaimed, "Permanently closed", wrong phone and PIN. Until it's fixed, RealWaves can't appear in the map pack or "near me" results.
5. Google's AI Overviews already read the website for brand questions, and for local category questions they recommend businesses with Google Maps listings and reviews.
