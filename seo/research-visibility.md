**Tools and their reliability**

- **Brave Search** (`search.brave.com`, `country=in`) gave real, localized result pages for 9 queries. After that it returned HTTP 429 (rate limit) for every request. It is the main source for the keyword table.
- **WebSearch tool:** a US-only index. The order of its links is **not** a Google India rank. I used it as a fallback, and the table labels it "WS-US".
- **Bing via WebFetch** (`cc=IN&setlang=en-IN`) returned a plausible page for only one query ("Real Waves Packs Raipur"). For 8 others it returned unrelated pages such as dictionary entries, MSFT stock quotes and ChatGPT threads. That looks like degraded results served to bots, so those pages are treated as **not usable**.
- **Google** (`google.com/search`) returned an error page ("having trouble accessing Google Search"). **DuckDuckGo HTML** showed a CAPTCHA, which I did not attempt. **Startpage** could not be fetched, **Ecosia** and **Mojeek** returned 403, **Yahoo** returned 500, and **Qwant** said "not available in your country".
- **Geolocation:** the network I fetched from geolocates to Bihar. Brave localized one query to Patna, and Google Maps and the "near me" screenshot centred on Muzaffarpur. "Near me" results are therefore **not** representative of Raipur.

## 5. Key takeaways

1. **The Google Maps listing is probably the biggest local-visibility blocker.** It is named "RealWeaves Packs(India)Pvt. Ltd." (misspelled), uses PIN 492001 and a landline, and, per the evidence screenshot, is **marked "Permanently closed" and shows "Claim this business"**. The pin may also not be at Saddu. RW appears in none of the Google, Bing or Brave map packs I saw, while competitors with 3 to 129 reviews do. Claim the profile, mark it open, and fix the name, phone, category and pin.
2. **The live site tells search engines the Vercel URL is the real site.** The canonical, sitemap, robots `Sitemap:` line, JSON-LD and OG images all point to `real-waves-website.vercel.app`. That domain serves an identical copy with no redirect, and Google ranks the vercel.app URL first for the brand query. /about, /products and /blogs canonicalize to the homepage. The uncommitted code appears to fix this. Once deployed, add a 301/308 redirect from vercel.app to www, change the apex redirect from 307 to 308, and resubmit the sitemap in Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
3. **Non-brand visibility is very low, and directories own the SERPs.** IndiaMART, TradeIndia, JustDial and ExportersIndia fill most of the top 5 for every product query. On Brave, RW appeared for only 3 of 9 queries: box bag #3, non-woven manufacturer about #6, and D-cut #8 (the vercel.app URL). It appeared for none of the WS-US queries.
4. **NAP and name are inconsistent everywhere.** There are 7 name variants, 2 PINs, 3 landmark phrasings, 2 JustDial listings in different localities, and 2 different registered addresses on MCA aggregators. Choose one public name (for example "Realwaves Packs (India) Pvt. Ltd." with "RealWaves" as the brand) and one address, PIN and phone, then make every profile match.
5. **The directory profiles that exist are thin.** IndiaMART has no street address and no website link. Both JustDial listings have 0 reviews. There is no TradeIndia, ExportersIndia, LinkedIn or YouTube presence, and Instagram has 581 followers. Completing IndiaMART and JustDial (address, website, hours, photos), merging the duplicate JustDial listing, and collecting reviews are cheap wins. Google's AI Overview already pulls from the site and IndiaMART for the brand query.

## 6. Limitations

- **No direct Google results.** Google's search page returned an error, so all Google SERP, AI Overview and Maps-status data comes from screenshots made by another process. I did not reproduce them. The Maps name, address and plus code are corroborated by the redirect I resolved; the "Permanently closed" status, phone number and "claim" prompt are not.
- **Brave was rate-limited** (HTTP 429) after 9 queries, so queries 10 to 18 use the US-only WebSearch tool. Its order is not a Google India rank.
- **Bing via WebFetch was unreliable.** Only 1 of 9 queries returned a relevant page. DuckDuckGo showed a CAPTCHA, which I did not attempt. Startpage, Ecosia, Mojeek, Yahoo and Qwant were blocked or unavailable.
- **The network geolocates to Bihar.** "Near me" and unlocalized queries are not representative of Raipur.
- **JustDial, ZaubaCorp and Economic Times pages were blocked** (403 or unfetchable), so their details come from search snippets only.
- **Not checked:** Bing Places, Udyam, YouTube's native search, LinkedIn's native search, Sulekha, IndianYellowPages, Dial4Trade, and ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot answers. **No search-volume or rank-tracking data** was used, and no volumes are claimed.
