Part 1 · Technical

Google can now find and trust every page

These were the hidden problems that stopped Google from showing the website. All are fixed in the new version.

Every page can now be found and read

Fixed

In simple words: Google reads a page's text without clicking buttons. On the old website, the product list and blog posts only appeared after clicking or loading, so Google saw empty pages.

Before

Only 4 of 22 pages could be found. The product list showed grey boxes to Google.

What we changed

Made every product and blog card a real link, and put the full text in each page.

Result

All 27 pages can be found. Google can list them within a few weeks of going live.

Google is told the correct website address

Fixed1 setting for you

In simple words: every page had a hidden note telling Google "my real address is real-waves-website.vercel.app", a temporary copy. Google believed it, which is why the copy shows up first for your name.

Before

All 22 pages pointed to the temporary copy. Four pages also shared one title.

What we changed

Every page now points to its own address on realwavespacks.com. The temporary copy sends visitors to the real site.

Result

Within weeks, realwavespacks.com replaces the copy in Google.

Your one setting: in Vercel → Domains, set the redirect from realwavespacks.com to www as permanent (308) instead of temporary (307).

Business details Google can read

Fixed

In simple words: websites can include a hidden "business card" for Google with the address, phone and map location. The old one only said "Raipur".

Before

City only. No street, PIN or map location.

What we changed

Full Saddu address, PIN 492014, map location, both phones, email, legal company name, Facebook and Instagram.

Result

Google and AI tools understand who you are and where you are. Opening hours will be added once you confirm them.

Faster homepage on phones

Improved

In simple words: most buyers search on a phone. The homepage was slow because of a very large video and a picture that loaded last.

Before

Score 67 out of 100. The main picture took 9.9 seconds. The video alone was 20 MB.

What we changed

Shrank the video to 4 MB with no visible difference, made the main picture load first, and stopped it waiting for an animation before showing.

Result

Score 78. The main picture shows in 4.5 seconds. Other pages already scored 93–100.
