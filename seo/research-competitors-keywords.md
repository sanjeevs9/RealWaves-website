# Real Waves Packs: competitor, keyword and source research

Prepared: 9 Oct 2026. Every fact below was checked on 9 Oct 2026 unless another date is given.
Scope: read-only desk research using WebSearch, WebFetch, and Google's public autocomplete endpoint (fetched with WebFetch). No browser tools were used, and no forms or sign-ins. Where Real Waves' own rankings or listings came up by chance, they are flagged for the separate visibility workstream and were not looked into further.

**How to read the evidence (please read first)**
- WebSearch is US-based and returns AI-summarised result sets. **It does not show Google India rankings.** So "competitors that win visibility" here means sites that kept appearing for these queries in the tools, plus the marketplace pages that list Raipur sellers. This list is not a verified Google.co.in top 10.
- WebFetch pages are summarised by a small model. Prices, MOQs and GSMs taken from directory pages are **listed asking prices as shown on 9 Oct 2026**. Re-check any figure before using it in public copy.
- Structured data (JSON-LD) could not be checked, because WebFetch drops `<script>` blocks. The structured data column is therefore "not verified" for every site.
- Search volumes: **none are given.** Demand is qualitative only, based on whether Google autocomplete returned a suggestion.

### 3a. Central rules: Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2021

**Verified from the Gazette text** (Gazette of India Extraordinary, Part II Sec 3(i), MoEFCC notification **G.S.R. 571(E), 12 Aug 2021**). The copy read is hosted by the Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board: https://tnpcb.gov.in/owems/pdf/regulations/Plastic%20Waste%20Management%20Rules,%202016,%20(Second%20Amendments),%202021.pdf (the file name says "Second Amendments", but the content is G.S.R. 571(E)). Checked 9 Oct 2026.

| Rule | Exact text (brief) | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Definition, rule 3(na) | "'Non-woven plastic bag' means Non-woven plastic bag made up of plastic sheet or web structured fabric of entangled plastic fibers or filaments … bonded together by mechanical or thermal or chemical means" | Verified (gazette) |
| Minimum GSM, rule 4(1)(j) | "non-woven plastic carry bag shall not be less than 60 Gram Per Square Meter (GSM) with effect from the 30th September, 2021." | **Verified. Your belief (≥60 GSM from 30 Sept 2021) is correct.** |
| 240-micron alternative | Not in the notified text. The March 2021 draft (G.S.R. 169(E)) reportedly offered "60 GSM or 240 micron" ([India Environment Portal summary of draft](https://www.indiaenvironmentportal.org.in/reports-and-documents/draft-plastic-waste-management-amendment-rules-2021)) | The final rule is GSM-only (verified). Draft wording is from a secondary source. |
| Plastic carry-bag thickness, rule 4(1)(c) | "seventy five microns … with effect from the 30th September, 2021 and one hundred and twenty (120) microns … with effect from the 31st December, 2022" | Verified (gazette) |
| Single-use plastic ban, rule 4(2), from **1 July 2022** | (a) "ear buds with plastic sticks, plastic sticks for balloons, plastic flags, candy sticks, ice-cream sticks, polystyrene [Thermocol] for decoration"; (b) "plates, cups, glasses, cutlery such as forks, spoons, knives, straw, trays, wrapping or packing films around sweet boxes, invitation cards, and cigarette packets, plastic or PVC banners less than 100 micron, stirrers." Rule 4(3): (b) does not apply to compostable plastic. | Verified (gazette). The same list appears in PIB's explainer "Ban on Single Use Plastic in India" dated 1 Jul 2022: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2022/jul/doc20227169001.pdf |

**Government restatement (2024)**: PIB, MoEFCC, "Compulsory ban on polythene bags", 25 Jul 2024 (Rajya Sabha reply): "Non-woven plastic carry bags of less than 60 gram per sqm (GSM) are also prohibited from 30th September 2021." PRID 2036730 (https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2036730). Read from the copy annexed to an NGT filing: https://www.greentribunal.gov.in/sites/default/files/news_updates/Appeal%2056%20of%202025%20Affidavit%20file%20by%20KSPCB.pdf. The PIB page itself was not fetched directly.

**Later amendments (2024, 2025, 2026)**: secondary summaries describe compostable/biodegradable marking and certification (2024), barcode/QR labelling and penalties (Jan 2025), and recycled-content/reuse targets (2025–26). **None of the summaries I read mention a change to the 60 GSM rule.** These were *not* checked against the gazette. Sources: https://indiaenvironmentportal.org.in/reports-and-documents/plastic-waste-management-amendment-rules-2024 ; https://visionias.in/current-affairs/news-today/2026-04-04/environment/plastic-waste-management-amendment-rules-2026-notified-by-the-government

**Content implication**: under the law these are *plastic* bags (non-woven polypropylene) that are legal at ≥60 GSM. Copy should say "reusable, 60 GSM-compliant non-woven bags", **not** "plastic-free" or "biodegradable". Competitor listings of 20–40 GSM D-cut and W-cut "carry bags" (e.g. Eagle Bag 20 GSM; Kanha 30–40 GSM items; Karam 20–40 GSM W/U-cut) fall below the central minimum for carry bags. That is a fair, factual differentiator to state without naming anyone.

**What a small manufacturer can do** (my synthesis of the sources above):
1. Make sure `robots.txt`, the CDN/host and any firewall allow Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot. Blocking GPTBot (training) is optional and separate.
2. Put hard facts in **HTML text and tables**, not images or PDFs: GSM per bag type, size chart, capacity, MOQ, lead time, price-per-kg logic, factory address and the 60 GSM compliance statement. These are the "specific, measurable" facts AI answers quote.
3. Write question-led sections with 1–2 sentence direct answers ("Are non-woven bags banned in Chhattisgarh?", "What GSM should a sweet-box bag be?"), citing the gazette.
4. Keep entity data consistent everywhere: website, Google Business Profile, IndiaMART/Justdial/TradeIndia (Google's AI-features doc tells sites to keep Business Profile current).
5. Verify the site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, use IndexNow, and track `utm_source=chatgpt.com` and Bing AI Performance citations.
